About Grace Global Tech & Educational Consult

We are an education and technology-driven enterprise committed to empowering students and individuals with the knowledge, digital skills and opportunities they need to grow and make an impact.

We bridge education and technology with computer training, AI and digital skills, academic writing support, admission and educational consulting, scholarship and study-abroad guidance, and SIWES placement assistance.
